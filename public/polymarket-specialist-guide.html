Timeframes

The specialist heads now support these horizons:

1m 3m 5m 7m 10m 15m 30m

Important: only 5m and 15m are real Polymarket-style markets in the app. The other horizons are useful practice/evidence mirrors unless you have an actual matching market.

The default big-move buckets are horizon-aware: BTC_MOVE_BUCKETS_USD_BY_HORIZON=1:10|20|40;3:20|35|70;5:30|60|100;7:40|80|140;10:50|100|180;15:60|120|300;30:100|200|600. Each horizon stores meaningful | large | extreme boundaries. The first value is the binary training line; the full range helps the app explain whether movement is quiet, meaningful, large, or extreme.

Decision Flow

1. Feed stale? -> AVOID 2. Price sitting exactly at the line? -> WAIT 3. Big-move quiet and P(hold) not strong? -> WAIT 4. Big-drop HIGH and setup is UP? -> AVOID LONG 5. Big-drop HIGH, side is DOWN, model confirms DOWN, range supports? -> POSSIBLE DOWN (WATCH_DOWN) 6. Activity quiet and P(hold) below 93%? -> WAIT 7. Big-up/down conflicts with current side and P(hold) below 93%? -> WAIT 8. Model lean disagrees with the live side and P(hold) below 93%? -> WAIT 9. Side is UP, model confirms UP, big-up HIGH, room ok, P(hold) 85-93%? -> POSSIBLE UP (WATCH_UP) 10. P(hold) above 93%? -> SETUP, then check market price 11. Live ask is cheaper than fair value after buffer? -> PAPER_BET candidate 12. No edge after price/spread/costs? -> NO_EDGE / WAIT 13. (If trained) learned meta Champion below its gate? -> downgrade to WAIT

What The App Does Not Yet Prove

  • It does not prove profitable betting until live market ask/spread, fees, slippage, and resolved outcomes are measured.
  • It does not use ElasticNet/Ridge as a separate live app head yet. Those are research candidates.
  • The learned meta Champion is not trained yet. It needs at least hundreds of resolved Champion snapshots.
  • More history does not guarantee higher accuracy. BTC regimes decay; stale data can hurt.

One knob: set BTC_HISTORICAL_DAYS=180 (or 360) in start.bat. On the next launch it rebuilds the 1m research matrix to that window and every specialist head retrains on it automatically (the window is baked into each head version). Each head fits on 98% of the window and keeps the most recent 2% as an honest held-out test (BTC_TRAIN_SPLIT_FRAC). The first wide build downloads that many days of spot aggTrades once (cached after).

Practical recommendation: the tiny specialist heads handle 180/360 days comfortably; keep the heavy main ensemble on a smaller window and compare 180/360 offline first, promoting only heads that beat the current Champion on the held-out test and live-like validation.
