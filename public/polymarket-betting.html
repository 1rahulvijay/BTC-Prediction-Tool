⚡ The 30-second version

  1. Only bet when source = MODEL lean (never ⚠ WEAK / fallback — those are coin-flips).
  2. Prefer Grade A > B > skip C.
  3. The magnitude must project PAST the line — "expects drop of $40 → projects $30 below beat" is a real edge; "$8 drop, $20 to the line" is not.
  4. Skip CHOP / STRETCH path outlooks.
  5. The bet only profits if your win-rate > the share price you pay. 60% wins at 65¢ loses money.

DWeak / fallback lean SKIP

⚠ WEAK (fallback) means the model was near-neutral and the side is just a probability tilt. Measured ≈ coin-flip. No grade, no magnitude, no bet — regardless of how tempting the price looks.

FNO LEAN / CHOP / training SIT OUT
  • NO LEAN — no bet: the model is genuinely neutral. There is no play.
  • 🧭 CHOP outlook: "price will wobble ±$5 around the line, closing side near coin-flip." The line is exactly where the noise lives.
  • Model is training: during a retrain the model can't commit. Every window is NO LEAN. Wait for it to finish.

📐 Reading the magnitude forecast (new)

The model doesn't just pick a side — its move-size regressor forecasts how far price travels, with a confidence band (conformal low / median / high). The card shows:

📐 Model expects a drop of ≈ $40 (band $22–$61) → projects close $62,296 (−$30 vs beat → DOWN resolves)

How to use it

  • Edge = projected-vs-beat distance. The bigger the projection clears the line, the safer the directional bet.
  • The band is your risk. If the band's near edge ($22 here) still clears the line, the move is reliably big enough. If the band straddles the line, the magnitude is a coin-flip even when the sign is right → drop to Tier C.
  • Sign vs magnitude can disagree with "where it is now." Price can be on the UP side right now but the model expects a $40 drop — that's a fade setup; trust the projection over the current tick only on Grade A.
Coming in V5: this point-forecast becomes a true probability — P(close ≥ beat) — which maps directly to a fair share price. Until then, use the band-clears-line rule as the manual version of that math.

🚫 Bet-killers (instant skip, no matter what else looks good)

  • Fallback lean. ⚠ WEAK = coin-flip. The price can look like free money; it isn't.
  • CHOP outlook. The model is telling you the closing side is random. Believe it.
  • LOW_VOLATILITY regime. The weakest cell in live evidence — the line and the noise overlap.
  • Band straddles the line on a Grade-C setup. Right sign, wrong size, weak confirmation — three strikes.
  • Tiny sample win-rate. "model 100% (2)" means nothing. Wait for n≥100 before trusting the strip.
  • Paying above your edge. The single most common way to lose while being "right" — win-rate must beat the share price.

🧠 Honest math

  • 5-minute BTC direction is mostly noise. The current model's raw leans measure ~51% (coin-flip). The edge lives in the selected slice: model leans + Grade A + magnitude clearing the line + good regime. Selectivity is the whole game.
  • Win-rate is not profit. Profit = win-rate vs share price. Cheaper entry beats more predictions. Always.
  • Fewer bets = higher win-rate. Nobody wins 70% betting every window. You win 70% by betting the best 5–10% of windows and sitting out the rest.
  • The model is being retrained toward less bias (v5). Until the win-rate strip shows model-leans clearing 55% on a real sample, treat this as paper-tracking, not sized betting.
  • Not financial advice. Educational decision-support only. Never bet money you can't lose.
