⚡ The 30-second version

  1. Only bet when source = MODEL lean (never ⚠ WEAK / fallback — those are coin-flips, measured).
  2. If the lean is FLIPPING (changing UP↔DOWN every minute) → SKIP. A flip means the model is near-50/50; instability is the no-edge signal. Stable conviction = real signal. why →
  3. The magnitude must project PAST the line — "expects drop of $40 → projects $30 below beat" is real; "$8 drop, $20 to the line" is not. (It's a typical move, not a guarantee.)
  4. Skip CHOP / STRETCH outlooks, and skip Grade C.
  5. The bet only profits if your win-rate > the share price you pay. 60% wins at 65¢ loses money.

Reality check: 5m BTC direction is ~coin-flip for almost everyone. The edge is selectivity — bet only the few stable, high-grade, magnitude-clears windows; sit out the rest (which is most of them). Fewer bets, higher win rate. That's the whole game.

FNO LEAN / CHOP / training SIT OUT
  • NO LEAN — no bet: the model is genuinely neutral. There is no play.
  • 🧭 CHOP outlook: "price will wobble ±$5 around the line, closing side near coin-flip." The line is exactly where the noise lives.
  • Model is training: during a retrain the model can't commit. Every window is NO LEAN. Wait for it to finish.

🔄 In-window states — opened vs live

A bet locks at window-open, so the big header (direction · grade) is the snapshot at open and stays fixed. The card then tracks the live market in two places below the header — and those, not the header, tell you how the bet is doing:

WhereWhat it tells you
"Now: $… (±$X → UP/DOWN side)"Which side price is on right now, and by how much. If it's on the opposite side of your bet, you're currently losing.
"live lean now …" (small, amber)The model's current lean. If it differs from the header, the model has changed its mind since open.
The advice box (bordered)The lifecycle coach — see below.

So a header that says UP · Grade C while the live note says "live lean now DOWN" is not a contradiction — it's "you opened UP, the model has since flipped DOWN." The header is the photo at open; the live note is now.

The advice box is what to act on: HOLD (on-side, model agrees → let it ride) · LOCK IN (ahead but the lean faded → sell to bank the small win) · EXIT (losing / model flipped against you → cut it to limit loss) · HOLD/WAIT (mixed → watch). Read the advice box, not the frozen header, once a bet is live.

🌀 Why the lean flips — and why a flipping lean is a SKIP

You'll see a card open UP and minutes later read "live lean now DOWN." That is not the model being broken or indecisive — it's the model being honest about a coin-flip.

What's actually happening

  • On a weak window the model's true answer is NEUTRAL. The market still needs a side, so the card shows whichever way the probability tilts by a hair — 50.4% UP one moment, 49.6% the next.
  • The model re-predicts every few seconds on fresh price/flow. When the signal sits right at 50%, any tiny move pushes the tilt across the line. The "flip" is a near-zero number changing sign — there was never real conviction to abandon.
  • High-conviction calls do NOT flip. A Grade-A 65%-one-side lean is anchored far from 50% and holds steady. Only the weak ones flip.
The rule: a lean that flips is the model telling you "I don't know." That instability is the no-edge signal. If it's flipping, skip it. Stability is the fingerprint of a real signal — bet the calm, conviction leans; ignore the flippers (which is most 5m/15m windows).

📐 Reading the magnitude forecast

The model forecasts how far price typically travels, with a 50% band. The card shows:

📐 Typical drop ≈ $40 (50% band $22–$61) → projects close $62,296 (−$30 vs beat → DOWN resolves)

How to use it

  • It's a TYPICAL move, not a path guarantee. The label is the median for current conditions; a $100+ window lands outside the band ~50% of the time by design. Don't read it as "price will do exactly this."
  • Edge = projected-vs-beat distance. The further the projection clears the line, the safer the directional bet.
  • The band is your risk. If the band's near edge ($22 here) still clears the line, the move is reliably big enough. If the band straddles the line, the magnitude is a coin-flip even when the sign is right → drop to Tier C / skip.
  • Magnitude can disagree with "where it is now." Price can be on the UP side this second while the model expects a $40 drop — a fade. Trust the projection over the current tick only when the lean is stable and high-grade.
What this is NOT (yet): there is no "fair value in cents" / probability on the card. An earlier version showed one, but it was built on this flat typical-move number and was misleadingly precise, so it was removed. A real P(close ≥ beat) only returns once the magnitude estimate itself becomes trustworthy (a future retrain) — and only if it then proves out. No fake precision in the meantime.

🚫 Bet-killers (instant skip, no matter what else looks good)

  • Flipping lean. If it changes UP↔DOWN every minute, the model is at 50/50. Instability = no edge. Skip.
  • Fallback lean. ⚠ WEAK = coin-flip. The price can look like free money; it isn't.
  • CHOP / STRETCH outlook. The model is telling you the closing side is random, or the move can't reach the line. Believe it.
  • LOW_VOLATILITY regime. A weak cell in live evidence — the line and the noise overlap.
  • Band straddles the line. Right sign, wrong size — the magnitude is a coin-flip even when the direction isn't.
  • Tiny sample win-rate. "model 100% (2)" means nothing. Wait for n≥100 before trusting the strip.
  • Paying above your edge. The single most common way to lose while being "right" — win-rate must beat the share price.

🧠 Honest math

  • 5-minute BTC direction is mostly noise. Every model family in the ensemble independently lands at ~51% out-of-sample — that's not a broken model, it's the honest ceiling of 5m direction on these inputs. Tuning or bigger models won't move it; only genuinely new information can.
  • The edge lives in the SELECTED slice, not every call. Some cells are real (calm-market 3m/10m have shown ~54–56%). You don't need 55% everywhere — you need it on the few windows you act on: stable model leans + magnitude clearing the line + good regime, and skip the rest.
  • Win-rate is not profit. Profit = win-rate vs share price. Cheaper entry beats more predictions. Always. 60% wins at 65¢ loses money.
  • Fewer bets = higher win-rate. Nobody sustains 70% at 5m. You get a high win-rate by betting the best 5–10% of windows and sitting out the other 90%+. The app is a filter, not an oracle.
  • Until the strip shows model-leans clearing ~55% at n≥100, treat this as PAPER-TRACKING, not sized betting. The model is mid-measurement.
  • Not financial advice. Educational decision-support only. Never bet money you can't lose.
