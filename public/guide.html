⭐ The 3 golden rules

  1. Bet the LEAN, not the action. The app shows two things: what the model thinks (the lean: UP/DOWN) and whether it would trade it (the action: BUY/SELL/WAIT). For Polymarket, the lean is your product. WAIT does not mean "the model doesn't know" — it means "not worth a leveraged trade after fees".
  2. Fewer bets = higher win rate. Nobody wins 80% betting every window. You win 80% by only betting the best 5–10% of windows. Every tool below exists to tell you which windows those are.
  3. Trust measured numbers, not feelings. Every win-rate shown is from real, resolved predictions. If a panel says 64%, that's what actually happened — no backtest tricks.

🆕 What changed (2026-06-11) — read this first

  • The grading correction (the big one). A deep audit found the internal hit column meant two opposite things: "the call was right" on committed calls, but "good thing we didn't trade" on gated ones. Several win rates this guide previously quoted (model leans ~64%, grades 70–82%) were inflated by that artifact and are retracted. Every accuracy number is now graded by sign-truth: did the lean match the realized move's direction. The ladder below shows the honest numbers.
  • The honest baseline: the current (v3) model's raw leans measure ~45–52% — coin-flip — across all horizons. The selectivity machinery (grades, regimes, late-entry) is real, but its precision claims must be re-measured on clean grading.
  • The v4 retrain is the fix attempt: 130 features including new trend-persistence features (so the model can stop fading uptrends — its measured worst habit), regime experts trained on the same regime partition they serve, and several gate bugs fixed. It trains automatically on the next app start (~5h).
  • The price-to-beat anchor moved to Pyth (tracks Polymarket's Chainlink settlement within a few $). Your win/loss record now matches what the market would actually pay. If Pyth stalls, the fallback price is offset-corrected into Pyth units, so mid-round venue mixing can't mis-grade a round.
  • Two new dedicated tabs — 📈 Binance and 🎯 Polymarket (below).

🎯 The Polymarket panel (Price-to-Beat) — field by field

Reference price to beat

The BTC price locked at the start of the 5m/15m window. The market resolves UP if the end price ≥ this number, DOWN otherwise. Ties go UP. Same rule Polymarket uses.

Our call (UP / DOWN)

The side the model favors for this window. Check its source:
model leanThe model genuinely committed to a direction. Still the better class of call — but on clean sign-truth grading they currently measure ~51% at 5m (v3). They become bettable only when the live strip shows them clearing ~55%.
[Weak lean] in the adviceThe model was near-neutral; the side is just a slight probability tilt. ~Coin-flip. Skip these.

The win-rate strip — "model 51% (201) | all 49% (399)"

Two win rates on purpose: "model" counts only real model leans, "all" includes the weak fallback calls (diluted). Judge the app by the model number — and only trust it once the sample size in parentheses is 100+. The strip is live: after the v4 retrain it re-earns its numbers from scratch.

Setup grade — [Setup grade A (4/5 confirmations)]

How many independent things agree with the call (see grades below). A = bet-worthy. B = decent. C = skip.

🧭 Path outlook — "how will price travel vs the line"

A plain-English forecast of the price journey, not just the ending side:
HOLDPrice is already on the model's side. Expect wobbles that test the line, but it should hold to the close.
CROSSPrice is on the wrong side, but the model expects enough movement to wobble here first, then cross the line and finish on its side.
STRETCHThe model has a lean, but expects less movement than the distance to the line — making it is a stretch. Skip or wait for price to come closer.
CHOPNo real drift expected — oscillation around the line, coin-flip close. Skip.
The odds quoted ("Measured odds for this setup: 71%") come from real past outcomes in the same situation — not guesses.

The live advice box (while a window is open)

HOLDYou're ahead and the model still agrees. Let it settle.
LOCK INYou're ahead but the model's lean faded. Consider selling your shares now to keep the profit.
HOLD / WAITYou're behind but the model still believes. Reversal possible — hold unless it worsens.
EXITYou're behind AND the model flipped. Cut the loss early.
LATE-ENTRY WINDOWThe highest-odds moment in the whole app. Less than 2 minutes left, price already clearly on one side, model agrees. Buying that side now is a bet that nothing reverses in 2 minutes — usually 80%+. Trade-off: the shares cost more, so you earn less per win.
Also in the box: Now vs beat (how far price is above/below the reference right now), Model now (current lean — can change mid-window), Exp. move (how big a move the model expects).

✅ The decision recipe (do this, in order)

  1. Open the Polymarket panel at the start of a 5m window (or watch for late-entry).
  2. Is the call a real model lean? If the advice says [Weak lean] → stop, skip this window.
  3. Check the setup grade. A → strong candidate. B → ok if you want more action. C → skip.
  4. Check the regime (Technical tab). LOW_VOLATILITY = the model's weakest spot (~58%) → prefer skipping. RANGE / TRENDING = its best (~73–87%).
  5. Check expectedPrecision if shown — that's the measured win-rate for this exact situation. Under 60%? Skip.
  6. Bet small and fixed. Same stake every time. Never chase a loss with a bigger bet.
  7. While the window runs, follow the advice box — HOLD / LOCK IN / EXIT are computed from live data.
  8. Prefer late-entry when available: if you see the LATE-ENTRY flag and shares of that side cost < ~85¢, that's the best risk/reward moment of the window.

🚫 Automatic "do not bet" signs

  • Advice says [Weak lean — best skipped] → the model basically said "I don't know".
  • Setup grade C → almost nothing confirms the call.
  • Regime = LOW_VOLATILITY → historically the model's coin-flip zone.
  • The win-rate strip "model" number is under ~58% for that horizon → the edge is too thin to beat typical share prices.
  • The feed just reconnected / app just restarted → give it ~30–60 min to warm up (order-flow features and VPIN need live data to fill).
  • You're emotional after a loss → the math doesn't care; bad bets after losses are how bankrolls die.

🏷️ Setup grades, exactly

Each call gets up to 5 checkmarks:

  1. ✔ The lean is a real model lean (not a weak fallback tilt)
  2. ✔ The regime is one the model is good in (not LOW_VOLATILITY)
  3. ✔ CVD agrees — net trade flow pushes the same way
  4. ✔ Large trades agree — whale flow pushes the same way
  5. ✔ Order book agrees — resting liquidity tilts the same way
Grade A4–5 checks → the high-precision subset. These are your bets.
Grade B3 checks → decent; fine if you want more volume.
Grade C0–2 checks → noise territory. Skip.

🧠 Honest expectations (read once, remember forever)

  • 5-minute BTC direction is mostly noise. A real, durable edge here is 58–65% — that's genuinely good. 80%+ exists only on a tiny, carefully-selected slice of windows.
  • Win rate isn't profit. On Polymarket, profit = win rate vs share price. Winning 64% but paying 70¢ a share loses money. Winning 64% paying 55¢ prints. Cheaper entry beats more predictions.
  • Small samples lie. A win-rate over 20 bets means little; over 200 it means something. The app shows the sample size next to every percentage — read it.
  • The model improves in steps, not magic: more uptime → more recorded data → calibration activates → the gate picks better bets → retrain with richer features. Patience is a feature.
  • Never bet money you can't lose. This is decision support, not a money printer.
